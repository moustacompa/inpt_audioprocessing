" they later received the approbation of gregory xiii. st charles died in 1584. these oblates were dispersed by napoleon i in 1810, while another group called the oblates of our lady of rho escaped this fate. in 1848 they were reorganized and given the name of " oblates of st. charles " and reassigned the house of the holy sepulchre. in the course of the 19th century similar groups were founded in a number of countries, including the " oblates of st charles ", established in london by cardinal nicholas wiseman. see also st. ambrose university references anabaptism baptism premillennialism
