minute sub - orbital flight aboard freedom 7. the first american to orbit the earth was john glenn, aboard friendship 7 on 20 february 1962. the first american woman in space was sally ride, during space shuttle challenger ' s mission sts - 7, on 18 june 1983. in 1992, mae jemison became the first african american woman to travel in space aboard sts - 47. cosmonaut alexei leonov was the first person to conduct an extravehicular activity ( eva ), ( commonly called a " spacewalk " ), on 18 march 1965, on the soviet union ' s voskhod 2 mission. this was followed two and a half months later by astronaut ed white who made the first american eva on nasa ' s gemini 4 mission. the first crewed mission to orbit the moon, apollo 8, included american william anders who was born in hong kong, making him the first asian -
