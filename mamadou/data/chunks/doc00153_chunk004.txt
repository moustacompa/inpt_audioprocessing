van vogt would have no association with scientology, as he did not approve of its mysticism. the california dianetics operation went broke nine months later, but never went bankrupt, due to van vogt ' s arrangements with creditors. shortly afterward, van vogt and his wife opened their own dianetics center, partly financed by his writings, until he " signed off " around 1961. from 1951 until 1961, van vogt ' s focus was on dianetics, and no new story ideas flowed from his typewriter. fix - ups however, during the 1950s, van vogt retrospectively patched together many of his previously published stories into novels, sometimes creating new interstitial material to help bridge gaps in the narrative. van vogt referred to the resulting books as " fix - ups ", a term that entered the vocabulary of science - fiction criticism. when the original stories were closely related this was often successful, although some van vogt fix - ups featured disparate stories thrown together that bore little relation to each other, generally making for a less coherent plot. one of his best - known ( and well - regarded ) novels, the voyage of the space beagle ( 1950 ) was a fix - up of four short stories including " discord in scarlet " ;
