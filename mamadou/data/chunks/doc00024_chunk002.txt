oriented information such as written language. before ascii was developed, the encodings in use included 26 alphabetic characters, 10 numerical digits, and from 11 to 25 special graphic symbols. to include all these, and control characters compatible with the comite consultatif international telephonique et telegraphique ( ccitt ) international telegraph alphabet no. 2 ( ita2 ) standard of 1924, fieldata ( 1956 ), and early ebcdic ( 1963 ), more than 64 codes were required for ascii. ita2 was in turn based on the 5 -
