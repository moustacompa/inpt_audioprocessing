exists, emigrant families appear to be even less likely to return. the emigration of the major part of the armenian population has brought about important changes. for example, a decrease in the number of people of reproductive age in armenia has led to a progressive drop in marriages and birth rates. there has also been a considerable change in the ethnic composition of the population in
