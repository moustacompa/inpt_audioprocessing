, entities considered angels in judaism are invoked as gods alongside abrasax : thus " i conjure you... by the god michael, by the god souriel, by the god gabriel, by the god raphael, by the god abrasax ablathanalba akrammachari... ". in text pgm v. 96 - 172, abrasax is identified as part of the " true name which has been transmitted to the prophets of israel " of the " headless one, who created heaven and earth, who created night and day... osoronnophris whom none has ever seen... awesome and invisible god with an empty spirit " ; the name also includes iao and adonai. " osoronnophris " represents egyptian wsir wn - nfr, " osiris the perfect being ". another identification with osiris is made in pgm vii. 643 - 51 : " you are not wine, but the guts of osiris, the guts of... ablanathanalba akrammachamarei eee, who has been stationed over necessity, iakoub ia iao sabaoth adonai abrasax. " pgm viii. 1 - 63, on the other hand, identifies abrasax as a name of " hermes " ( i. e. thoth ). here the numerological properties of the name are invoked, with its seven letters corresponding to the seven planets and its isopsephic value of 365 corresponding to the days of the year. thoth is also identified with abrasax in pgm lxxix. 1 - 7 : " i am the soul of darkness, abrasax, the eternal one, michael, but my true name is thoouth, thoouth.
