century of angolan civil war to become the fastest - growing economy in africa and one of the fastest - growing in the world, with an average gdp growth of 20 % between 2005 and 2007. in the period 2001 – 10, angola had the world ' s highest annual average gdp growth, at 11. 1 %. in 2004, the exim bank of china approved a $ 2 billion line of credit to angola, to be used for rebuilding angola ' s infrastructure, and to limit the influence of the international monetary fund there. china is angola ' s biggest trade partner and export destination as well as the fourth -
