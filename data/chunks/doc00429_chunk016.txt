of the two incipient species. this idea came to be known as the wallace effect, later called reinforcement. wallace had suggested to darwin that natural selection could play a role in preventing hybridisation in private correspondence as early as 1868, but had not worked it out to this level of detail. it continues to be a topic of research in evolutionary biology today, with both computer
